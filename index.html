Carbohydrates

4 kcal / gram

Slow-digesting. Oats, rice, sweet potato — sustained energy for training.

Simple sugars

Fast energy. Fruit, honey, dextrose — ideal immediately post-workout.

Non-digestible. Feeds gut bacteria, slows sugar absorption, keeps you full.

Water & Hydration

0 kcal / gram
💪 Strength output
🌡️ Temperature
🔄 Nutrient transport
🧠 Focus

A 2% drop in body water reduces strength by up to 10% and endurance by 20%.

Daily baseline

35 ml × bodyweight in kg. (82kg = 2.87L minimum)

Training days

Add 500–750 ml per hour of training. Sip electrolytes.
